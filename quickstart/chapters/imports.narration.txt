# Home screen and importing blocks (vanilla Minecraft, then a GTNH modpack).

[home]
This is home base: projects, palettes, prefabs, and libraries. Libraries first, because that's where your blocks live.

[empty]
It's empty right now, so let's fill it. Hit Add blocks.

[vanilla]
Point it at your own game files. Here's a Minecraft 1.21 jar. voxyl reads the models and textures straight from your install, and never ships any Minecraft content of its own.

[search]
Search for what you want, and import.

[vanilla_done]
And there they are. Each mod gets its own library.

[gtnh]
Playing a modpack? voxyl speaks NEI. In game, open NEI, then Options, Tools, Data Dumps. Dump the items, and the item panel in CSV mode.

[gtnh_pick]
Then choose the NEI roster format and point voxyl at your instance. Nearly nineteen thousand blocks, found.

[gtnh_import]
Add a prefix like G T N H to keep things tidy, search for Korp, and import.

[done]
Libraries, sorted by mod, ready for any palette.
