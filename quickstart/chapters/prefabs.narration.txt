# Prefabs: save a region once, place it anywhere.

[intro]
Built something worth reusing? Save it as a prefab.

[save]
Select it, press Control P, give it a name and a few tags. The preview shows exactly what you're keeping, and you can untick any semantic to leave it out.

[home]
Prefabs live in your own library, and they're shown through the palettes they were made with.

[rotate]
Drag a prefab to turn it around, and scroll to zoom in.

[place]
To place one, open the inventory and pick it. It's just like pasting from before.

[repeat]
Right-click to drop it. It stays live, so keep placing more, and press Escape when you're done. A whole factory floor in a few clicks.
