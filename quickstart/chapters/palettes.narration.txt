# Semantic blocks and palettes: the core idea.

[hotbar]
Look at the hotbar. It holds your building blocks, but not directly. Instead of stone or planks, you give each one a semantic meaning, like Floor, Wall, or Accent, and decide what block that is later.

[meaning]
You build with a palette, not with blocks. You can assign a block to each palette entry later.

[palette]
A palette decides what each meaning looks like. In this one, Channel Glow is a glowing cyan slab.

[edit]
Change your mind about an entry? Right-click it, pick a different block, and every voxel using it updates instantly.

[edit_done]
There, we just changed every glow channel all at once, by editing its palette entry.

[shapes]
And entries aren't limited to whole blocks. Voxyl supports ArchitectureCraft shapes and Forge Microblocks too.

[swap]
Or swap the whole look. Add a different palette to the project.

[swap_result]
Same voxels. Completely different build.

[swap2]
And another.

[principle]
Not a single block was modified. You can build structure first, and decide on the look later.
