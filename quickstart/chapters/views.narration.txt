# Views, and flying around.

[intro]
Every build can have as many views as you like, and they're all live.

[slice]
Press Tab to slice through it. Tab again cycles the axis, W and S slide the plane, and Enter opens it as a flat 2D view, right beside the 3D one.

[edit]
Paint in the slice, and the 3D view changes right along with it. One build, many lenses.

[layers]
Step through the layers with the arrows, and turn the slice to taste.

[fly]
Back in 3D, click to fly. W, A, S and D to move, Space to rise, Shift to sink. Tap Control while you move to sprint, and Escape to let go.

[more]
And two is just the start. Add a second 3D view from another angle, and a second slice from another axis.

[clay]
Every 3D view has its own render mode. Try Clay, for pure form. Or intent, x-ray, outline, wireframe. There are plenty to choose from.

[orbit]
Then switch on Orbit, and that view circles your build all by itself. Work in one view while another shows you every side.

[end]
Same build, whichever way you want to look at it.
