# Views, and flying around.

[intro]
Projects can have many views of the same scene, and you can edit blocks in any of them.

[slice]
To create a 2D view of the scene, press Tab in the 3D view, and you'll be selecting a slice. Tab again cycles which axis, W and S slide the plane, and Enter opens it as a flat 2D view, right beside the 3D one.

[edit]
Paint in the slice, and the 3D view changes right along with it. One build, many lenses.

[layers]
Step through the layers with the arrows, and rotate the view to taste.

[fly]
Back in 3D, click to fly. W, A, S and D to move, Space to rise, Shift to sink. Tap Control while you move to sprint. Hit Escape to exit fly mode.

[more]
You can have more than 2 views, and you can arrange them however you like. For example, add a second 3D view from another angle, and a second slice from another axis.

[clay]
Every 3D view has its own render mode. Try Clay, for pure form. Or intent, x-ray, outline, wireframe. There are plenty to choose from.

[orbit]
Then switch on Orbit, and that view circles your build all by itself. Work in one view while another shows you every side.

[end]
Same build, whichever way you want to look at it.
