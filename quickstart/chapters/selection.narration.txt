# Selection tool. One "[id] line" per spoken beat; a blank line ends a beat.
# The same text becomes the voice-over, the burned-in captions and the SRT.
# (Odd words are respelled for the voice only, in lexicon.txt: captions keep the real spelling.)

[intro]
Selection is the tool you'll reach for all day. And it does a lot more than draw a box.

[fly]
Click into the view and you're flying. Let's rise up for a better angle.

[tool]
Press E for your inventory, then pick the Select tool.

[corners]
Right-click two opposite corners on the ground, and you've claimed a footprint.

[panel]
Middle-click, and its panel opens. You get the exact dimensions straight away, which makes this a pretty handy tape measure too.

[nudge]
Now pull the top up to the height you want. Hold Shift to move it five cells at a time, and watch the numbers. Seventeen by eighteen by seventeen. That's the whole pillar.

[semantics]
Below that, what's inside, by meaning. Every semantic, and how many of each.

[blocks]
Flip to Blocks, and it's the real shopping list: the actual blocks, with stack counts. Copy it, and you're ready to gather.

[agent_intro]
Now the fun part. Your agent speaks semantics, so you can just say what you mean.

[agent1]
Only the glowing channels? One sentence.

[agent2]
Everything except the glow and the plinth? Another.

[agent_outro]
No coordinates. No counting. It knows what your blocks mean.

[ops]
And once you have a selection, you can copy it, cut it, delete it, cut it away to peek inside, save it as a prefab, or export it for Schematica.
